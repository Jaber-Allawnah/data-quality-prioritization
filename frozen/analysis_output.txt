DEFINITIONS — the three columns use DIFFERENT row sets, by design:
  Damage_pp / Net_Improvement_pp   mean over ALL rows for the error type
  Eligible_*                       mean over rows with damage >= 0.5pp only
  Conditional_Recovery_%           SUM(improvement) / SUM(damage) over those
                                   eligible rows == Eligible_Improvement_pp / Eligible_Damage_pp
  Coverage_%                       share of rows that were eligible

  Net_Improvement_pp / Damage_pp does NOT equal Conditional_Recovery_%,
  because the first two average over every row including undamaged ones,
  while the ratio is computed only where damage was material.
